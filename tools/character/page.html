The mannequin

The site's fighter: a wooden figure, as an artist's mannequin, drawn here by its own code (packages/three/src/mannequin.ts, painted by materials.ts). Its pieces were shaped from the five reference views below, measured row by row; their lengths are GrappleMap's, so any position in the database poses it. Pick reference wood to see it as the reference, or one of the site's looks.

Proportions

Medians over every GrappleMap position. Each varies by less than 0.5 cm: GrappleMap has a single body, so this one T-pose describes every figure in it.

The hand

The palm runs from Wrist to Hand; the four fingers, together, from Hand on, folding there by the angle GrappleMap stores. The thumb is not in GrappleMap: for now its fold follows the fingers' (a closing hand closes the thumb). It can become data later, for grips like thumb-in.

What it is made of

wood: head, neck, chest, abdomen, pelvis, upper arms, forearms, thighs, shins, feet, palms, four fingers and two thumb pieces a hand29
joints: balls in shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles, neck and thumbs, the waist filler, the knuckle pins, the seams at wrists, ankles and neck25
belt band and knot2

56 rigid pieces, each placed straight from the joints every frame; nothing deforms. The joint balls fill every bend.

The reference

The five views the new mannequin is shaped from. Click one to look at the model from the same side. Each piece's width and depth were measured from these silhouettes, row by row; lengths stay GrappleMap's, so the model's arms and legs keep the database's proportions (the reference's arm span is about 2.0 m, GrappleMap's 1.79 m).

Rebuilding it

  1. The previous character (rigid tubes, blocks and balls), in a T-pose.
  2. A new mannequin: sculpted segments, joint discs, a two-digit hand with a thumb.
  3. The new mannequin shaped from the reference's five views, piece by piece.
  4. Joints filled: a wooden ball in each shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle and the neck, a filler at the waist, a pin at the knuckles and balls in the thumb, so a bent joint closes instead of opening a gap. The waist is straight: the pelvis and the abdomen lean half-way to the line from mid-hip to neck, since GrappleMap's Core sits behind it.
  5. The wooden figure replaces the previous character in packages/three: the site, the Storybook and this page draw it.
