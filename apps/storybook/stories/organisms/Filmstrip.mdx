# Filmstrip

The player under the move. It replaces a timeline with something people already understand:
**a real frame of every step**.

<Canvas of={Filmstrip.Playing} />
<Controls of={Filmstrip.Playing} />

## Its parts, from top to bottom

- **The risk line**, red, only while a lock is in its danger range: what can happen to the
  joint ("From here the lock can injure the elbow").
- **The caption**: the step being played ("3. Sit close to the shoulder…") or the move's name.
- **The strip**: ▶ / ❚❚, one cell per step, and the **next move** at the end, which continues
  the fight from where this one ends.

## Moving through time

- **Click a cell** to jump to its step (and pause).
- **Drag across** the strip to scrub: under the pointer, the time runs from the cell's step to
  the next one.
- On the site, **space** plays and pauses and the **arrows** step.

<Canvas of={Filmstrip.Paused} />

```ts
import { filmstrip } from "@bjj3d/ui";

const f = filmstrip({ onPlay, onPick: (step) => …, onScrub: (time) => … });
f.setSteps(stops, viewer.thumbnails(stops), viewer.last);
f.setNext({ href, label: "next", title, onClick, onMore });
// every frame:
f.setTime(viewer.time, viewer.playing, { play: "Play", pause: "Pause" });
```
