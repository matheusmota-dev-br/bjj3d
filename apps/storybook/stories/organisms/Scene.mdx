# Scene

Two fighters in a place, with their lights. A place sets the floor and what surrounds it,
the background and fog, how far the camera may go, and how strong the sky light is.

## The built-in places

All of them are drawn in code (canvas textures and planes); nothing is downloaded.

**Mat**: a competition mat, 1 m tiles, an 8 × 8 m area with a safety border.

**Gym**: the mat in a room with padded walls. The camera stays inside the room.

<Canvas of={Scene.Gym} />

**Studio**: an endless backdrop of one colour for floor, horizon and sky; only the shadows
show depth. The cleanest place to read a technique.

## Light and dark

Each place has a light and a dark version (the toolbar's theme). A place tells the looks
whether it is bright (`light`), because the x-ray look adds light on dark backgrounds and
tints on bright ones.

## A new place

A place is a `PlaceDef`: an id and a `build(theme)` that returns the `Environment` (a group
of objects, background, fog, `maxDistance`, `light`, `hemi`).

```ts
const viewer = new Viewer(canvas, { places: [competition] });
viewer.setEnvironment("competition", "dark");
```

When the place changes, the viewer disposes the old one's geometry and materials. See
**Guides/A new place, look or belt**.
