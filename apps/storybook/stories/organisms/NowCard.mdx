# Now card

The top of the panel: what is on screen, explained.

<Canvas of={NowCard.Technique} />

## Its parts

- **A picture**, which follows the filmstrip: it always shows the step being played.
- **The name and what it is** ("Move · from A to B").
- **Who moves**, with the fighter's dot. GrappleMap marks some moves as done by the top or the
  bottom player; the card says which of the two people on screen that is, even after a chain
  swapped them.
- **The steps**, for a reviewed technique. Click a step to jump to it; the step being played is
  highlighted.
- **Notes**, and "Draft text, not reviewed yet" until someone who knows the technique has read
  it.

A position has only a name and "Position".
