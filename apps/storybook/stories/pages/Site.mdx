# Site

The real app, `apps/web`, from its production build: the viewer with the interface around
it. Each story is the site at one address.

The build is made when Storybook starts, if there is none; after changing the site,
`pnpm build` refreshes it.

## First visit

The armbar from knee on belly, a big "Oss!" and the short "how to use".

<Canvas of={Site.FirstVisit} />

## A shared link

Addresses open a move directly: `#t/<n>` a transition (`#t/<n>r` backwards), `#p/<n>` a
position, and `?frame=<k>` opens paused at keyframe *k*. This one is the armbar, paused at
keyframe 6.

<Canvas of={Site.SharedLink} />

## A position

A position shows what can be done from it: the moves that start there, each with a picture
of where it ends.

<Canvas of={Site.Position} />

## What is around the viewer

- **The move fills the screen.** Under it, a filmstrip with a real frame of every step:
  click to jump, drag to scrub; the last cell offers the next move.
- **Browse** slides the panel over the view: what is on screen, next moves, techniques,
  finishes and positions.
- **⚙** holds the look, the place, the other cameras, the speed and the options.
- **Fighters**: build and belt of each one, on a screen of their own.
- 16 languages; Portuguese and English reviewed.
