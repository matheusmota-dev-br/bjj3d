# Metrics

One reading per submission kind: how far the attacked joint is pushed through its range,
from geometry alone. Each story plays a finish of that kind; open the **Reading** panel.

<Controls of={Metrics.Elbow} />

| kind | reading | danger from |
|---|---|---|
| elbow lock | elbow flexion, with the opponent's hips on the elbow | under 14° |
| knee bar | knee flexion, with the opponent's hips on the knee | under 25° |
| shoulder lock (kimura, omoplata) | internal rotation of the upper arm | over 110° |
| knee twist (heel hook, toe hold) | rotation of the shin under the bent knee | over 60° |
| choke | gap between the throat and the opponent's second-closest limb | under 12.2 cm |
| neck crank | neck flexion | over 75° |

These are angles and distances, not forces: GrappleMap has neither forces nor timing.

## Elbow

The arm nearly straight **and** the opponent's hips near the elbow; a straight arm alone is
not an armbar.

<Canvas of={Metrics.Elbow} />

## Shoulder

Rotation of the upper arm about its own axis. The forearm only shows it when the elbow is
bent, so the reading carries a confidence that fades as the arm straightens. Grappling arms
live in internal rotation (77° median outside submissions), so only well past that counts.

<Canvas of={Metrics.Shoulder} />

## Knee

Like the elbow: the leg nearly straight with the opponent's hips on the knee.

<Canvas of={Metrics.Knee} />

## Knee twist

How far the foot turns away from where the thigh points, under a bent knee.

<Canvas of={Metrics.KneeTwist} />

## Choke

A choke needs the neck held from two sides (forearm and biceps, or thigh and shin), so the
reading is the distance to the opponent's **second**-closest limb. With bodies at full
thickness a forearm cannot get closer than about 9 cm to the neck's axis.

<Canvas of={Metrics.Choke} />

## Neck crank

Flexion of the neck, from the core through the neck to the head.

<Canvas of={Metrics.NeckCrank} />

## A registry

Each kind is a `SubmissionMetric` (tags, side, danger threshold, `read`, `glowPoint`) and
`createMetrics([...builtinMetrics, yours])` makes a registry the viewer takes. `npm run
validate` measures every kind in the registry over the whole database and writes
`docs/validation.md`. See **Guides/A new submission**.
