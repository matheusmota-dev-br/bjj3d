# Finishes

Every finishing transition in the database (named "tap" or "finish", with a submission
kind), each at its last frame, with its kind, name and line in `GrappleMap.txt`.

<Canvas of={Finishes.Gallery} />

## What to look for

Each finish should end with its joint lit. `docs/validation.md` has the numbers: 15 of the
20 reach the danger range, and for the other five it says why (in each case the GrappleMap
animation never gets there: an arm left straight, a limb that stays far from the throat).
When you change a metric, this page is the quick look and `pnpm validate` the count.

## Many viewers on one page

There are more finishes here than a browser allows WebGL contexts (about 16). Each cell
makes its viewer only while it is on screen and disposes it when it scrolls away, so
scroll and the cells come and go.
