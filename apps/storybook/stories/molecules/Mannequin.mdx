# Mannequin

One fighter. A rigid, rounded piece between each pair of GrappleMap joints, a ball at each
joint, blocks for the torso, a plain head with a small nose (it shows where the head
faces), simple hands and feet, and a belt.

## Nothing deforms

Every piece is placed straight from the 23 joints, every frame. There is no skin, no
skinning and no rig: the figure *is* the data. If a limb looks wrong, the pose is wrong,
and that is something you want to see.

## Thickness and the contact solver

GrappleMap's figures are stick figures. Given thickness, two of them would sink into each
other. The pieces are exactly as thick as the capsules of the contact solver
(`@bjj3d/core/contact`, sizes in `data/body/capsules.json`), so what the solver keeps
apart is what you see kept apart.

**Build** scales the pieces from slim (0.8) to sturdy (1.2). The solver is tuned for the
standard build (1), so a much sturdier fighter may overlap a little more.

## The data underneath

Turn on **sticks** to draw GrappleMap's stick figure over the mannequin: the lines are the
solved poses, the same numbers the mannequin is placed from.
