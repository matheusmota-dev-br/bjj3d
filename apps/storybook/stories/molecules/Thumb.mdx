# Thumb

One cell of the filmstrip: **a real frame** of a step of the move (drawn by the viewer, not a
picture made by hand), the step's number, and a bar that fills while that step plays.

<Canvas of={Thumb.Thumb} />
<Controls of={Thumb.Thumb} />

The step being played is outlined (`now`); the steps before it are full.

A step is a keyframe, or, for a reviewed technique, one of its written steps (which may span
several keyframes). The filmstrip (**Organisms/Interface/Filmstrip**) makes and updates the
cells; a page never needs to touch them.

```ts
import { thumb } from "@bjj3d/ui";
thumb(2, src); // the third step
```
