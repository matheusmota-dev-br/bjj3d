# Ref row

One row of the panel's lists: a position, a move or a finish. Every list in the panel (next
moves, techniques, finishes, positions, search results, "moves into this position") is made
of these rows.

<Canvas of={RefRow.Finish} />
<Controls of={RefRow.Finish} />

## What it shows

- **A picture of where it ends**: the last frame of a move, or the position. People
  recognize positions faster than they read their names. On the site the pictures are drawn
  only when the row scrolls into view.
- **The name**, then the submission badge if the move finishes with one.
- **A line saying what it is**: "Move · from A to B".

<Canvas of={RefRow.Position} />

## Next and here

In the **Next** list a row continues the fight from where the current move ended, so it shows
→ and, when clicked, the viewer chains it (the bodies stay where they are).

<Canvas of={RefRow.Next} />
