# Badge, dot, swatch and eyebrow

Four tiny atoms that say *what kind of thing* is next to them.

## Kind badge

The submission a move ends in: "Elbow lock", "Choke". It is red, the colour of the load on a
joint, and it only appears on moves that finish with a submission: in the title card, on
the panel's rows and on the now card's picture.

<Canvas of={Badge.KindBadge} />

## Fighter dot

A fighter's colour before a name: "● Amber moves", the fighter tabs. Amber and blue always
mean the same two people (see **Foundations/Tokens**).

<Canvas of={Badge.FighterDots} />

## Swatch

A colour sample on a choice, as on the belt buttons.

<Canvas of={Badge.Swatches} />

## Eyebrow

The small capitalized label over each section of the panel: NOW, NEXT, EXPLORE. It names
the section; it is never a heading people read as content.

<Canvas of={Badge.Eyebrow} />
